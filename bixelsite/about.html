What I do

Six capabilities, one designer

01

Brand identity

Positioning, marks, type and colour systems, and guidelines a team can apply without me in the room.

02

Product design

Mobile and web interfaces designed in every real state — empty, filled, loading, interrupted — not only the version that photographs well.

03

Design systems

Components, tokens and documentation, so the tenth screen costs less to build than the first.

04

Web design

Sites planned around what the business needs a visitor to understand, then designed to say it in the first five seconds.

05

WordPress build

Custom themes, hand-built. No page builders, no template licences for the client to renew.

06

Art direction & motion

Campaign imagery, environmental design and logo animation that keep a brand recognisable at every scale.

Values

  • Strategy before aesthetics — the mark follows the position, not the other way around
  • First impressions matter — what appears in the first five seconds carries the most weight
  • Every screen in every state — not just the one that photographs well
  • I like to keep things simple for everyone
  • One system, not three handovers — brand and product designed together
  • Typography is a tool, not decoration
